# Spicy Peanut Butter Noodles

*noodles, peanut, spicy, vegan, quick*

**4 Servings**

---

- *16 oz* brown rice noodles (or any noodle)
- *3* large garlic cloves, minced
- *1 T* fresh ginger, grated
- *1 T* oil (olive, avocado, or coconut)
- *2/3 C* creamy peanut butter (low/no salt)
- *4 T* low sodium tamari or soy sauce
- *1/3-1 C* hot water
- *1 T* rice vinegar
- *1 T* toasted sesame oil
- *1-2 T* sriracha
- *1-2 T* maple syrup or honey
- *1/2 t* red pepper flakes
- *2 t* Chili Garlic Sauce (page \pageref{chile-garlic-sauce}) (optional)
- Garnish: crushed peanuts, scallions, cilantro, red pepper flakes

---

Cook noodles according to package directions. Drain.

In a large skillet or wok over low heat, cook garlic in oil until golden, about 2 minutes. Add ginger and cook 1 more minute. Remove from heat.

Add peanut butter, tamari, 1/3 cup hot water, rice vinegar, sesame oil, sriracha, maple syrup, red pepper flakes, and chili garlic sauce (if using). Whisk until smooth, adding more hot water as needed to reach desired consistency. Taste and adjust.

Add about half the sauce to the noodles and toss, adding more to your preference. Garnish and serve warm or cold. Keeps in the fridge for 5 days; revive with a splash of water or broth when reheating.
